"Foodie Favorites: Discover Local Delicacies"

VADA PAV

Vada Pav is a beloved street food from Maharashtra, featuring a spicy potato fritter sandwiched between a soft bread roll, often served with chutneys and fried green chili.

PRICE : RS.10 - RS.20

MISAL PAV

Misal Pav is a spicy Maharashtrian dish featuring a flavorful curry made from sprouted lentils, served with soft bread rolls, making it a beloved street food staple in the region.

PRICE : RS.50 - RS.70

PURAN POLI

Puran Poli is a traditional Maharashtrian sweet flatbread filled with a delectable mixture of jaggery and yellow split peas, often enjoyed during festivals and special occasions.

PRICE : RS.70 - RS.100
